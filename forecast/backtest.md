## Question and method

- **Question.** Does AR(1) forecast the terminal Cooling and Excess scores better than climatology and a constant 0.5? (forecast quality only; pricing evaluated separately)
- **Origins.** 1965-01..2021-01 monthly (673); each forecast covers the next 48 months.
- **Fit.** OLS y_{t+1} = a + b y_t + e on observations dated HIST_START .. t0-1 (macrolib.fit_ou(until=t0-1); its slice includes `until`).
- **Simulation.** 10000 paths x 48 months from x0 = y_(t0-1); Gaussian innovations with the fitted residual sd (ddof=2); x0 not part of the contract path.
- **Settlement maths.** simulated percent -> floor(y * 1e4) contract units, then the exact integer recursions and scores of eqs. (2)-(6). The contract statistics start afresh at the first forecast month; the previous month only starts the AR(1) recursion.
- **Climatology.** realised scores of every 48-month window that ended <= t0-1, from HIST_START.
- **Constant.** point mass at score 0.5 (CRPS = |0.5 - y|).
- **CRPS.** empirical-distribution CRPS: mean|X-y| - sum_i (2i-n-1) X_(i) / n^2 (pair term normalised 1/(2n^2)); 'fair' 1/(2n(n-1)) reported alongside.
- **Calibration.** randomised PIT u = F(y-) + v (F(y) - F(y-)), seeded v; coverage of central 50% / 90% ranges; point masses at 0 and at the cap.
- **Decision rule (declared before running).** Declared before running: a winner needs the 95% block-bootstrap CI of the mean CRPS
    difference to exclude 0 on the same side for every L in (24, 48, 96), seed-to-seed spread of
    the mean difference below 10% of its size, and the same sign in the non-overlapping origins.
- **Policy A.** fixed caps 8 pp / 80 pp-months: RETROSPECTIVE DEMONSTRATION (caps chosen after seeing 2021-24), not a pre-specified evaluation.
- **Policy B.** pre-origin percentile caps, rule below; applied to simulated, realised and climatology scores at the same origin.
  - statistic: 90th percentile of the exact integer statistic over eligible 48-month windows
  - eligible windows: start >= 1960-01 (declared post-1960 rule) and end <= t0 - 1
  - interpolation: linear between order statistics (Hyndman-Fan type 7; numpy 'linear', the pandas Series.quantile default used by research/build_notebook.py)
  - rounding: round half up to an integer in contract units (declared here: the repo has no integer rounding rule)
  - no valid cap: a percentile <= 0 gives no valid cap (the program needs cap > 0 and the notebook skips such products): that product is excluded from policy B at that origin and counted
  - source: research/README.md ('p90 ... post-1960 (or series-start) windows'), research/build_notebook.py (CAP_WINDOW_START = 1960-01, stats.quantile(0.9))

## Limitations

- About 14 independent 48-month blocks: average CRPS differences are noisy, and the bootstrap intervals are wide.
- The AR(1) fit window is not pinned down by the sources: the notebook fits on all history, the maths note's reference on 1997–2020. The primary (declared) uses all history; the 288-month variant shows the other reading.
- Policy A's caps were chosen after seeing 2021–24. Policy B's early origins rest on few eligible windows, and Excess has no valid B cap until enough post-1960 windows show any excess.
- Data timing: the forecast at t0 uses CPI for t0 − 1, which is published in the middle of month t0.
- The AR(1) is fitted to year-on-year inflation, an overlapping 12-month sum, so its residuals are serially correlated by construction. Gaussian innovations understate the tails seen in the residuals.
- Climatology starts in 1914, including both world wars and the 1920–21 and 1930s deflations. The 1960-start sensitivity shows how much that matters.
- The team's actual quote code (`forecast/ar1.py`) is not in the repository, so the pricing section evaluates the declared policy formula, not that code.
- The FRED snapshot has an empty value for 2025-10 (that month's CPI was not published). This backtest needs nothing after 2024-12, but any future 48-month window that spans that month cannot be computed from FRED as is.
